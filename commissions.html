Commissions

Information & Guidelines

My commissions follow a simple 5-step process:

1

Request

Fill out the Commission form with your project brief, preferred deadline, and contact details.

2

Contract & Pricing

If your request is accepted, you will receive an email with the final price (or price range), and a digital contract to sign for our mutual protection.

3

Deposit

Once everything is set for work to begin in your turn, a 25% non-refundable upfront deposit is required. Work begins as soon as both the signed contract and deposit are confirmed.

4

Drafts & Revisions

You will receive watermarked pitches/ drafts for feedback and minor adjustments within the scope of our agreed brief.

5

Final Delivery

Upon final approval, the remaining 75% balance (plus any requested add-ons) is paid, and you will receive your clean, unwatermarked high-resolution file(s).

Additional Payment & Fees

Project File Add-on

10€ - 30€ variable depending on commission level and asset complexity.

Rush Fee (Tight Deadline)

If you need your project moved up in my queue or delivered on a short deadline, a rush fee will be added depending on urgency and my schedule, capped at a maximum 25% fee of the base price.

Major Scope Change Fee

If mid-project you request changes that stray significantly from our agreed brief, and I have the availability and skills to accept them, a percentage fee will be calculated based on the extra work required, capped at 25% unless it elevates to a higher commission level. You will receive an updated quote to approve before any extra work begins. If declined, we can either stick to the original brief or proceed with cancellation as outlined in my policies.

Project Restart Fee

If a project is placed on hold due to client inactivity (14+ consecutive days without prior notice) and you wish to reactivate it, a fee up to 15% of the base price may apply to resume work, subject to queue availability.
